# Catchment Change Watch — School catchment & capacity tracker for Canada

A standalone product, not a demo. It watches what is *changing* about school catchments, with a source and an as-of date on every figure, and proposed clearly separated from decided.

## What it covers — live now: British Columbia, Victoria / Westshore

- **SD62 Sooke Catchment & Pathway Review (live):** Phase 2 survey Nov 23–Dec 4, 2026; open houses Jan 25 & 28, 2027; board decision Spring 2027; new Langford secondary, 2,000 students, opening Fall 2030. SD62 utilisation 117%.
- **SD61 Victoria:** 49 public schools, 21,154 students, 91% of operating capacity overall — but Tillicum 123%, Victoria High 120%, Eagle View 119%.
- **Utilisation calculator:** type in enrolment, operating capacity and nominal capacity — it shows both percentages, the gap, and spare seats under each reading, with a live result line under the blanks and meter bars. Presets for Cunningham (71.6% / 64.8%), Weir (90.0% / 81.5%), MacCorkindale (66.2% / 60.0%), and Queen Alexandra (142 / 262, 54.2%). Preset seat counts are examples reproducing the published percentages.
- **Queen Alexandra brief:** known facts (142 enrolled, 262 capacity, 54% full, H3 seismic, ~2,000 new homes nearby, Broadway Subway Fall 2027), what a decision still needs (cost, funder, catchment impact — none published), and a clearly labelled scenario table (not a forecast).
- **Freshness audit:** stale SD61 facility data, capacity coverage (951 of 1,569 public schools), missing source lines, and generated-summary errors.
- **Territory brief:** a sample one-page brief a realtor can use in a client conversation, plus a plain sizing tool in dissemination areas (it prices nothing).

## Coverage roadmap

British Columbia (live) · Alberta · Ontario · Quebec (French-first). No enrolment, capacity, or boundary figures are claimed for Alberta, Ontario, or Quebec until they are sourced from those provinces' own publications.

How a new province gets added: source inventory, definitions first, standard Census geography (dissemination areas), freshness audit on day one, no invented boundaries.
