# Catchment Change Watch — Victoria / Westshore (SD61 + SD62)

A clean, send-ready tool — not a pitch — for The Visual Thinking Company (VTCo).

It watches what is *changing* about school catchments in Greater Victoria, with a source and an as-of date on every figure, and proposed clearly separated from decided.

## What it covers

- **SD62 Sooke Catchment & Pathway Review (live):** Phase 2 survey Nov 23–Dec 4, 2026; open houses Jan 25 & 28, 2027; board decision Spring 2027; new Langford secondary, 2,000 students, opening Fall 2030. SD62 utilisation 117%.
- **SD61 Victoria:** 49 public schools, 21,154 students, 91% of operating capacity overall — but Tillicum 123%, Victoria High 120%, Eagle View 119%.
- **Utilisation calculator:** type in enrolment, operating capacity and nominal capacity — it shows both percentages, the gap, and spare seats under each reading. Presets for Cunningham (71.6% / 64.8%), Weir (90.0% / 81.5%), MacCorkindale (66.2% / 60.0%), and Queen Alexandra (142 / 262, 54.2%). Preset seat counts are examples reproducing the published percentages.
- **Queen Alexandra brief:** known facts (142 enrolled, 262 capacity, 54% full, H3 seismic, ~2,000 new homes nearby, Broadway Subway Fall 2027), what a decision still needs (cost, funder, catchment impact — none published), and a clearly labelled scenario table (not a forecast).
- **Freshness audit of School Guide:** stale SD61 facility data, capacity coverage (951 of 1,569 public schools), missing source lines in text fetch, and the healthcare gap VTCo named on its Nov 19, 2026 blog.
- **LinkedIn findings (Nov 21, 2026, signed-in read-only check):** Vik Khanna repeats "We are hiring a combination of data scientists and social domain experts" in Oct–Nov 2026 posts, while the VTCo company page says "No jobs posted."
- **Territory Brief:** a sample realtor-ready one-pager, and a Territory Intelligence estimator at $7 per dissemination area per month (illustrative, not a forecast).
